Questions

What owners usually ask.

What do I need to give you?

Your answers to our questionnaire, plus photos, menu or service details, hours, and contact information. We handle the design and the build.

Who owns my website?

You do. You can still ask us to make edits, and we keep it maintained.

What if something breaks or I want a change?

Tell us through the report form or by email. Maintenance is part of your monthly fee, and we also talk through updates at your regular check-ins.

Will my site look like everyone else’s?

No. We design each site around your industry, your style, and your wishes.

Do I have to be technical?

No. You tell us what you want and we build it.

Which businesses do you work with?

Right now, local restaurants and contractors. We plan to add other types of business later.
