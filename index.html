Why not a template

A template makes every business look the same.

If customers cannot find you online, they call someone they can. Plenty of good local businesses still have no website at all.

Many of the ones that do use a template or an AI builder. The layout is the same as every other business on it, and the owner has little say in how it looks.

We start by learning your trade, your style, and what you want customers to do. Then we build your site around that.

Who we build for

Two kinds of business. Two different kinds of site.

A restaurant site and a contractor site have different jobs, so they are not built the same way.

Restaurants

A site that makes people hungry and makes it easy to order, reserve or ask about catering.

  • Your menu as real text, with photos
  • Hours, location and a clear call or order button
  • Catering and party requests

Contractors

A site that shows your work and turns a visit into a call or a quote request.

  • One page for each main service
  • Photos of finished jobs and your service area
  • A quote request and a call button on every page
