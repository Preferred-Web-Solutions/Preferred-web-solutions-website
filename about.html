About Preferred Web Solutions

A Cincinnati company that builds custom websites for restaurants and contractors.

Why we started

Plenty of good local businesses have no website, or have one that looks like everyone else’s. Customers who cannot find you online call someone they can.

Preferred Web Solutions exists to fix that. We design each site around the business behind it: its trade, its style and what the owner wants customers to do. You have a say in how it looks, and you own the result.

We also stay after launch. We maintain your site, deal with problems when they come up, and sit down with you every 3 to 6 months to look at how it is doing.

Meet the owner

I’m Steven Embry, the owner of Preferred Web Solutions. I live in the Cincinnati area, and I started this company because so many good local restaurants and contractors either have no website or have one that looks like every other business on the same builder.

I build each site myself, and I’m the person you deal with from the first conversation to every check-in after launch. No hand-offs and no call center. If something on your site needs fixing, you email me and I reply within one business day.

My goal is simple: give you a site that is made for your business, that you own, and that helps customers find you and reach you.

How we work

Three things we promise.

  • Built for your businessEvery site starts from your trade, your colors, your photos and your words, not from a preset.
  • No templatesWe do not reuse another business’s layout. A diner, a fine-dining room and a roofer each get a site that fits them.
  • Owned by youThe site is yours. You can still ask us to make edits, and we keep it maintained.
