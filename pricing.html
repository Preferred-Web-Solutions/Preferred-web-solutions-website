Questions

Questions about pricing.

Is the sample site really free?

Yes. We show you a sample before you commit to anything. You only pay if you decide to go ahead.

When do I pay each fee?

The build fee is paid once you sign the service agreement, before we start building. The go-live fee and your first monthly payment are due when you approve the finished site and are ready to launch.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Keeping your site maintained, handling problems when they come up, and a check-in every 3 to 6 months to look at how the site is doing. If something breaks, email preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com and we reply within one business day.

What does a founding client agree to?

Founding pricing is for our first five clients. In return, you give us a short written testimonial and let us show your site as an example of our work.

Is there a written agreement?

Yes. Every client signs a service agreement that sets out what is included, the fees and how payments work, before any work starts.
