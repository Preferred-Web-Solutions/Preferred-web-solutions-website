How it works and pricing

A free sample first. Then a one-time build fee, a go-live fee, and a monthly fee for maintenance and check-ins.

The process

Four steps from first call to launch.

  1. See a free sample

    Tell us about your business and we show you what your site could look like, before you commit to anything.

  2. Tell us your style

    You fill out a short questionnaire about your colors, your photos and what you want your site to do.

  3. Review and launch

    We build your site from your answers and meet to go through the draft. We make changes and launch when you are happy.

  4. We keep it up

    Maintenance is covered by your monthly fee, with a check-in every 3 to 6 months to look at how the site is doing and plan updates.

Questions

What owners usually ask.

What do I need to give you?

Your answers to our short questionnaire, plus photos, your menu or service details, hours, and contact information. We handle the design and the build.

Who owns my website?

You do. You can still ask us to make edits, and we keep it maintained.

What if something breaks or I want a change?

Email us at preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com or use the report form. Maintenance is part of your monthly fee, and we also talk through updates at your regular check-ins.

Will my site look like everyone else’s?

No. We design each site around your trade, your style, and your wishes. We do not use templates.

Do I have to be technical?

No. You tell us what you want and we build it.

Which businesses do you work with?

Restaurants and contractors, starting in the Cincinnati area. We also work with businesses in other cities, since your site is built online. Something else? Send a note and we will tell you if we can help.
