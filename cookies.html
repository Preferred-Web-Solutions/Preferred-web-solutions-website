Cookie policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated October 6, 2026

This page lists everything this website stores on your device, so you can see exactly what happens when you visit.

What this site stores

This site sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for one item:

ItemWherePurposeCategoryLastsSet by
pws-consentLocal storageRemembers your choice on the privacy banner: policy version, time, and your choicesEssentialUntil you clear it or the policy version changesThis site

What this site does not do

  • No analytics or statistics tools.
  • No advertising or marketing pixels.
  • No embedded maps, videos, or social media widgets.
  • No fonts or scripts loaded from other companies’ servers. Fonts are served from this site.

Your details go to our form service, Formspree, only when you press send on a form.

Changing your choice

Use the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page to reopen the banner and change your choice at any time. Because we use nothing beyond the essential item above, your choices on analytics and marketing have no effect today. If we add those tools later, they will stay off until you opt in.

Clearing what is stored

You can clear stored site data in your browser: in Chrome, Edge and Firefox open Settings, then Privacy, then clear browsing data or site data. In Safari, open Settings, then Privacy, then manage website data. Clearing it will show the banner again on your next visit.
