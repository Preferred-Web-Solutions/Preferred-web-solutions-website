Accessibility statement

Version 1.0 · Last updated November 5, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website. We build it with accessibility in mind and aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA. We cannot promise it is perfect, and we do not claim full compliance.

What we do

  • Every page has one main heading, headings in order, and landmarks for the header, navigation, main content and footer.
  • You can use the whole site with a keyboard. A skip link jumps past the menu, and the focus outline is always visible.
  • Text is real text, not pictures of text, and meets contrast guidelines. The page works when zoomed to 200 percent.
  • Images that carry meaning have descriptive text. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Form fields have labels, and messages after sending are announced to screen readers.
  • Nothing plays sound or moves on its own, and animations respect your device’s reduce-motion setting.
  • Buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall on phones.

How we check

Before each update we run an automated accessibility checker (axe) on every page and test with a keyboard. Automated tools find only part of the problems, so we also fix what people report to us.

Known limits

The sample sites linked from this website are separate design samples for fictional businesses. They are being updated to the same standard.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, please report an issue or email preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com. Tell us the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within one business day.
