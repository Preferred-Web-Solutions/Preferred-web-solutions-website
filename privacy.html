Privacy policy

Version 1.1 · Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Preferred Web Solutions collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Preferred Web Solutions designs and maintains websites for restaurants and contractors. You can reach us at preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the forms on this site:

  • Contact form: your name, business name, email address, phone number if you add one, type of business, city if you add one, and any message you write.
  • Report an issue form: your name and email if you add them, and the details you give us about the problem.
  • Email: anything you send to our address.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this site, and we do not collect payment details here.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, to talk about a website for your business, and to fix problems you report. We do not sell your information.

Who handles it

Form messages are processed by Formspree, a form service, and delivered to us by email. Formspree receives what you submit in order to pass it on to us. Your email provider also handles messages you send us directly.

How long we keep it

We keep messages for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records. Ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. Email preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com and we will respond.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site sets no cookies. It stores one item on your device to remember your banner choice. Details are in our cookie policy.

Children

This site is meant for business owners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the version number and date above. If a change affects the choices you made on our banner, we will ask again.

Contact

Questions about privacy: preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com.
